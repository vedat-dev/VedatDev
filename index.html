How to play Klondike Solitaire

Move all 52 cards to the four foundation piles, starting with Ace and ending with King.

  • Build tableau columns in descending order, alternating red and black.
  • Only Kings can be placed in empty tableau columns.
  • Click the stock pile to draw cards. Click the empty stock to recycle the waste.
  • Move Aces to foundations first, then build each suit upward.
  • Click a card, then click its destination. You can also drag cards.

Keyboard shortcuts

  • H — Show a possible move.
  • J — Move a selected card to a foundation when possible.
  • U — Undo the last move.
  • N — Start a new game.
  • Escape — Close a dialog or return to the menu.
