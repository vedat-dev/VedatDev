MINESWEEPER HELP

HOW TO PLAY

AVOID BOMBS
Some squares hide bombs. Reveal one and you lose.
READ NUMBERS
A number tells you how many bombs touch that square. A 1 means one nearby bomb.
PLACE FLAGS
Turn FLAG MODE on, then tap a square you think contains a bomb. Tap again to remove the flag.
WIN
Reveal every square without a bomb to win.
TIP: START WITH A CORNER OR AN OPEN AREA.
