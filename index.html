How to play

Move all 52 cards to the four foundation piles, starting with Ace and ending with King.

  • Build columns downward, alternating red and black.
  • Only Kings can go into empty columns.
  • Click the stock to draw cards. Click an empty stock to recycle.
  • Click a card, then its destination. Dragging is also supported.

Keyboard: H = Hint, J = Auto move, U = Undo, N = New Game, Escape = Close dialog.
